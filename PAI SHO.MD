18 Basic flowers -> 3 van elke soort    Harmony                                 Clashes
    - Rose -> Red 3                     White Jade && Chrysanthenum             Jasmin
    - Chrysantium -> Red 4              Rose && Rhodondron                      Lily
    - Rododendron -> Red 5              Jasmine && Chrysanthenum                White Jade
    - Jasmin White -> White 3           Rodondendron && Lily                    Rose
    - Lily -> White 4                   Jasmine White && White Jade             Chrystanthenum
    - White Jade -> White 5             Rose && Lily                            Rododendron
8 accent -> 2 van elke soort
    - Rock
        - Cancels all harmonizing in its path, cannot be moved by the wheel
    - Wheel
        - Moves all surrounding tiles 1 space clockwise except rocks
    - Knotwheat
        - Stops all harmonizing in its surrounding tiles
    - Boat
        - Cancels any accent tile
        - Switches places with flower and flower on 1 of old surrounding boat place 
2 Special -> 1 van elke soort
    - White Lotus
        - 2 spaces
        - Harmonizes with all
    - Orchid
        - 6 spaces
        - traps surrounding flowers
